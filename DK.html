Karachi's folklore and intangible heritage are fading, and few young people encounter these stories.

To make folklore accessible we developed an AR platform that preserves folklore.

Using the Unity engine and Vuforia plugin, we built marker-based and location-based AR.

Karsaz Bride Marker

Designed a zine of AR markers.

To enable marker-based AR unique markers were designed and compiled in a zine.

This was done for selected stories & locations:

1. The Ghost Bride of Karsaz Bride

2. Abdullah Shah Ghazi Shrine

3. Moriro and the Mach

4. Ram in Aram Bagh

5. The shrine and crocodiles of Mangopir
