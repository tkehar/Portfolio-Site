Speak Up

Brand Identity and Motion Design for a Youth Public-Speaking Program

Brand Identity · Logo Design · Motion Design · Logo Animation · Video Production · Social Media Content · Design for Children

Client:

Speak Up

Role:

Graphic Designer

Year:

2018

Team:

Mahnoor Mahar (Videographer)

A public speaking program mentoring children 8 to 12 needed a brand identity that would work on social media.

The logo was inspired by what a confident child looks like.

Having butterflies is often used to refer to a fear of public speaking. We instead used butterflies as a symbol for empowerment.

This motif was featured in animated posts for social media

Logo Variants

Produced a promotional video that generated awareness about the program and began enrollment
