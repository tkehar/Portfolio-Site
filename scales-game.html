🤲

Hand Tracking

SCALES uses your hands to navigate. In VR mode, your hands are tracked in real time. On desktop, click to interact.

Requires a WebXR-compatible headset for full hand tracking. Desktop mode uses mouse clicks.

🤏

Pinch to Select

Bring your index finger and thumb together — a pinch gesture — to select objects, confirm decisions, and navigate menus.

✋

Open Palm to Pause

Hold your palm flat and open to pause and view your current scores across the three zones: Ecology, Ethics, and Goals.

👆

Point to Explore

Point at any object in the world to read information about it. Your decisions in each zone ripple into the others.

⚖️

The Three Moments

Every decision in SCALES activates one of three imperial mechanics: MANAGE, DIFFERENCE, or INCLUDE. These mirror the apparatus of Empire as theorised by Hardt and Negri.
