Who Gets To Talk About Whom?

Exhibition Identity and Website Design

Exhibition Identity · Collateral Design · Web Design · Information Architecture · HTML/CSS · Designing within Constraints

Institution:

Gandhara Art Space

Role:

Communication Designer

Year:

2018

Team:

Hajra Haider Karrar (Curator) Fiza Khatri (Assistant Curator)

Context

An exhibition and discursive program held at Gandhara Art Space needed an identity and a website on a very small budget.

Tasks

Designed the exhibition collateral and promotional material for social media.

Actions

Structured the information architecture of website in two sections: exhibition and discursive, with resources sorted by theme. This was done to mirror the format of the exhibition.

Visit Website

Constraints

Built on Tumblr to keep costs at zero, writing custom HTML around the platform's limits.

Reflection

With no budget, the constraint became the brief. Tumblr’s limits pushed me to structure the site exactly like the exhibition, which made it easier to navigate. It was also my way into Karachi’s art community, and those relationships led directly to later projects like Look at the City from Here.
