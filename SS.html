A UAE-based client needed an app for retailers to order directly from manufacturers, delivered in under a week.

I worked as UX Lead and Product Designer at Salesflo, working with a product manager and a frontend engineer.

Built a design system and flows from familiar retail app patterns to move fast.

View Design System

Features

Used a company-code login to set up the retailer and manufacturer relationship from the first screen.

Adapted B2C browsing and product cards for carton and unit pricing.

Designed a checkout that shows delivery time and an itemised bill

Structured a loyalty program linked to order value which admins can switch off.

The app was designed and handed off within a week and led to the onboarding of pivotal client in the UAE.
