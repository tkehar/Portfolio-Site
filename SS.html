Sellorate B2B Retailer App

Design system and mobile ordering app for B2B retail

Design system · Component library · B2B e-commerce · Mobile app · Rapid delivery · Design leadership.

Company:

Salesflo

Role:

UX Lead

Year:

2023

Team:

Saman Farooq (Product Manager) Faseeh Abbasi (Product designer)

Context

Salesflo is a B2B SaaS platform that caters to FMCG companies creating visibility and documentation for teams working in field service, distribution, and retail.

Problem

A UAE-based client needed an app for retailers to order directly from manufacturers, delivered in under a week.

Competitor scans

We studied a mix of B2C and B2B retail apps to identify mental models that allowed us to structure flows in ways that would be familiar to the users.

Design system

We customized the design system by building on top of components from material design library. This allowed for rapid design and quick and easy development

View Design System

User flows

Company Code Login

Used a company-code login to set up the retailer and manufacturer relationship from the first screen.

B2B Search and Order

Adapted B2C browsing and product cards for carton and unit pricing.

Itemized bill and delivery status

Designed a checkout that shows delivery time and an itemized bill

Loyalty Program

Structured a loyalty program linked to order value which admins can switch off.

Impact

The app was designed and handed off within a week and led to the onboarding of a pivotal client in the UAE.

Reflection

This project taught me how to work rapidly and in constant coordination with the developer and with product team. I also learned that not every product needs to be developed from zero and relying on mental models can expedite the development of an early MVP that can be tested and improved.
