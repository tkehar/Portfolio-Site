Context

Sastaticket is an online travel agency that relies on its customer service team to handle large volumes of calls from customer queries to order confirmation. This team of 54 agents was the spine of the platform.

Problem

Customer service agents struggled to use a cluttered internal order system which lacked reliable and updated information on flight details, pricing errors and refunds. This had led to an average handle time of 4 mins per query which was too long given the amount of queries the team dealt with daily.

Task

I worked with the product team on discovery workshops, user research, information hierarchy & UI redesign.

Discovery workshops

We ran workshops with the customer service team to understand the major pain points of customer queries and what they struggled with in the current system.

  • Refund delays
  • Price miscalculations
  • No live flight data

User research

We conducted interviews and surveys with members of the CS team to flesh out their pain points and personas. The CS team consisted of about 54 agents and 4 managerial leads.

CS team survey results

A majority of the CS agents were aged 25-35. The most illuminating factor however was length of employment which revealed that approximately 25% of agents had been working for under 3 months, while over 47% had been working for over a year. Based on these findings we developed three personas within the CS Team.

< 3 months of experience

Struggle with workflow complexity, makes frequent calculation errors, Needs guardrails in the system to prevent mistakes.

1-2 years of experience

Struggle with multitasking, miscommunication and commitment. Frustrated with the limitations of the current OMS.

> 2 year of experience

Quietly built workarounds, cross-checking data for pricing, fare rules, and live flight information as the system never earned their trust.

Interface re-design

Using heat maps we identified the most interacted with features, and placed those in single header bar for each order. This practice informed our component design where we grouped actions and functions in terms of priority and similarity.

Design system

We developed a design system that presented order information clearly and used a system of color coding to highlight important data. A color coded system to help users differentiate between order statuses. This allowed the agents to understand at glance where in the journey the user is and how to better serve and guide them.
