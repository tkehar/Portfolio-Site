Customer service agents struggled to use a cluttered internal order system which lacked reliable data on pricing errors and refunds.

I worked with the product team on research, information hierarchy & UI.

Ran workshops to map major pain points.

• Refund delays

• Price miscalculations

• No live flight data

View Documentation

Survey of employment tenure for CS team

Surveyed agent tenure, identifying two user groups.

Agents with < 3 months of experience

Struggle with workflow complexity, makes frequent calculation errors, Needs guardrails in the system to prevent mistakes.

Agents with > 1 year of experience

Quietly built workarounds, Cross-checks data for pricing, fare rules, and live flight information. The system never earned their trust.

Used heatmaps to regroup elements by function & frequency of use.

Colour-coded order status to easily identify where a customer was in their journey.
