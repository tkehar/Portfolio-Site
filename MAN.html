Context

Makula is a CMMS and FSM platform for machinery manufacturers and distributors. The team was expanding its AI portfolio by automating tasks for its users.

Problem

A consistent complaint across clients was reporting for maintenance and field visits. Field service technicians made multiple facility visits a day and spent hours after documenting each causing delays, inconsistent reports and information gaps.

Stage Before Visit Arrival & Setup During Visit After Visit
Experience Confident Confident Stressed Overwhelmed
Objective
  • Find work orders for the day's visit.
  • Review machine history & previous service notes.
  • Confirm equipment to be serviced.
  • Take history from facility team.
  • Diagnose the problem.
  • Perform repairs or maintenance.
  • Write visit report.
  • Share with manager.
  • Share with facility.
Pain Points
  • Searching for logs or context on the previous visit.
  • Lack of stable Internet connection.
  • Important info missed or forgotten.
  • Visit interruptions.
  • Difficulty in notation or photography while repairing.
  • Time spent on manual notation.
  • Inconsistent reports & structure, Missing information.
  • Separated reports & work orders.
  • Lack of centralized documentation.

Task

I was tasked with developing an MVP for an AI notetaker. I conducted comp scans, led UI/UX design and prototyping for the MVP, working with the product team and a customer success team to iteratively improve the usability.

Competitor scans

We examined existing notetakers in the market to establish best practices and look for opportunities to develop USPs for our users.

User journey

Based on this we developed a revised user journey that addressed user pain points.

Features designed

Speech to text transcription

An audio notation flow with options to pause the recording and upload later when connected to Wifi.

Summary Types

Summary types that give structured outputs for common tasks and visit types to standardize reporting.

Image Capture

Image capture allows technicians to attach photos while recording for more detailed visit documentation.

Notes & Assets Linkage

Linked notes to assets, to centralize documentation and link it to part of the platform's operational record.

Custom Vocabulary

Added custom vocabulary so admins could improve transcription of domain specific terms.

Outcome

Users reported that the notetaker allowed them to continue their work while documenting it simultaneously. This automated the entire post-visit documentation stage, which typically took about 1-2 hours per day. It was a strong enough signal for the Makula team to offer 30 mins of free transcription to all app users. This was the first feature of the industrial AI module.
