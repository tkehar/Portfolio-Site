An online events ticketing platform in Pakistan needed a modern identity that appealed to audiences in socioeconomic classes A and B.

Explored cultural motifs, calligraphy and the ticket as a visual symbol.

Exploring the use of a ticket as a visual motif in the logo.
Variant in Urdu

Blended English and Urdu into a single "minglish" logo using a custom Kufic script and set a grid and type system around it.

The identity was used on the website, tickets, event bands and social media.
