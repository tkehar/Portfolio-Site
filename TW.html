Ticket Wala - Identity Design

Bilingual brand identity for an events ticketing platform

Brand identity · Bilingual typography · Urdu calligraphy · Custom Kufic logotype

Company:

Ticketwala

Role:

Graphic Designer

Year:

2019

Team:

-

Context

An online events ticketing platform in Pakistan needed a modern identity that appealed to audiences in socioeconomic classes A and B.

Concept

The name ticketwala is an amalgam of the word ticket and the Urdu word wala (the one who). Inspired by this amalgam and work I sketched and calligraphed ideas, exploring cultural motifs, calligraphy and the ticket as a visual symbol.

Grid and typography

Blended English and Urdu into a single "minglish" logo using a custom Kufic script and set a grid and type system around it. The final design removed the ticket motif, aligned the Urdu text for balance, and added a tagline for clarity.

Outcome

Two variants were provided to the brand for use on collateral they develop. A white version for dark backgrounds and black logo for lighter backgrounds to assure there is enough contrast. Longer variants of the logo were also produced for specific collaterals such as wrist bands, standees and web banners. The identity was used on the website, tickets, event bands and social media.

Reflection

The hardest call was cutting the ticket motif I started with. Once the Urdu and English letterforms carried the identity on their own, the ticket became redundant. It taught me to let typography do the work, especially in bilingual design, where each script has to hold its own weight.
